Method.

Eight steps run between the moment you press the button and the answer on screen. Most of them consult a public authority in real time. The whole round trip takes about three seconds.

01

Normalise your inputs

Every amount in another currency is converted to USD at European Central Bank reference rates before a single ratio is calculated.

European Central Bank
02

Locate you

Your ZIP becomes a city, a state and a pair of coordinates, which decides which institutions are in scope.

US postal geography
03

Price the money

Rates start from the Treasury's own reported average interest rate, then add a program spread and a credit-tier adjustment. The breakdown is printed on every program.

US Treasury
04

Run the rulebook

Credit score, loan-to-value, loan size and debt-to-income are tested against the published FHA, VA, USDA, Fannie Mae and SBA standards.

Federal standards
05

Find the real banks

Active insured institutions chartered in your state, plus every branch with a street address physically inside your ZIP code.

FDIC
06

Rank on service record

Each lender is scored on complaints per billion in assets, how many were about applying rather than repaying, and how often it answered on time.

CFPB
07

Date the money

The funding estimate counts business days and skips weekends and US bank holidays, so it never lands on a day the banks are shut.

Federal holiday calendar
08

Show the working

Every check reports your value, the threshold it met, and the federal authority that threshold comes from.

Basis

The rulebook.

Nine programs, each encoded from the document that governs it. Open any program in your results and the authority is printed at the bottom of the card.

  • FHA HUD Handbook 4000.1. Scores from 580 with 3.5 percent down, or from 500 with 10 percent. Debt-to-income to 43 percent, stretching to 50 with compensating factors.
  • VA VA Lenders Handbook M26-7. No down payment, no monthly mortgage insurance, and no VA-set credit floor. Basis applies the 580 overlay most lenders impose in practice.
  • USDA Rural Development Single Family Housing Guaranteed Loan Program. No down payment for moderate-income buyers inside eligible areas, typically from 640.
  • Conventional conforming Fannie Mae Selling Guide and the Freddie Mac equivalent. From 620, up to 97 percent loan-to-value, with mortgage insurance that falls away at 20 percent equity.
  • Jumbo Above the FHFA conforming limit and held on the lender's own books. Typically 700-plus with cash reserves and 10 to 20 percent down.
  • Auto, prime and credit-building Two tiers, from 660 and from 500, priced accordingly and capped on loan-to-value against the vehicle.
  • Personal, unsecured From 580, priced almost entirely on credit tier, with debt-to-income capped tighter than secured lending.
  • SBA 7(a) SBA SOP 50 10. Government-guaranteed business lending up to $5 million, which lets banks approve borrowers they would otherwise decline.

Precision.

Attaching a complaint record to the right bank is harder than it sounds. Two US government databases file the same institution under three different names, and none of them match.

FDIC charter

Manufacturers and Traders Trust Company

What the bank is legally called on its FDIC certificate.

FDIC holding company

M&T BANK CORP

The parent. Shares no words at all with the charter name.

CFPB company

M&T BANK CORPORATION

How complaints against it are filed. Upper case, exact match only.

Why it matters to you

A tool that gives up on this quietly shows every bank a clean record, which looks like good news and is not. Basis reconciles all three naming systems, and when an institution genuinely cannot be matched it says so plainly rather than handing it a record that belongs to somebody else. What you read against a bank's name is that bank's own history.

Built to stay up.

Several independent authorities feed one page. Basis is built so that no single one of them going down can take the answer away from you.

  • Independent failure Every source stands alone. Even if all of them were unreachable at once, your programs, your checks and the reasons behind them still come through.
  • Cached beats missing A recent cached answer is served in preference to losing a feature, and it is labelled as cached so you always know what you are looking at.
  • Honest degradation Any source that did not answer is named on your results, and the figures that depended on it are omitted rather than estimated.
  • Continuously measured Source health and match quality are tracked continuously and published openly, so nothing degrades quietly behind the scenes.

Good to know

Basis works from the credit score you enter and from published program rules. Individual lenders add their own criteria on top, and rates here are modelled from the current Treasury benchmark rather than quoted, so treat them as a well-founded opening position. Conforming and FHA dollar limits are national baselines and are revised each January.
