Private.

Basis needs no account, no email and no phone number, and it never asks for your name, address or social security number. What you type is used for one calculation and then discarded. Your financial figures never leave this server.

Never stored

Income, debt, credit score

Used to compute your ratios during the request, then dropped. There is no database in this application, nothing is written to disk, and nothing is written to a log.

Never sent out

Your financial figures

No outside service is told what you earn, what you owe or what your score is. No credit bureau is contacted. The whole calculation happens on this server.

Leaves the server

Your ZIP code

To find banks near you, the ZIP alone goes to the ZIP lookup and to the FDIC. It travels on its own, never alongside your financial details. Leave it blank and no location leaves at all, though you will not get a lender list.

Public data only

What gets cached

Responses from the public APIs are cached on disk so free services are not hammered. That cache holds bank records, exchange rates and holiday dates. It never holds anything about you.

What that means for you.

Four commitments, stated as what you get rather than how it is wired.

  • Nothing to sign up for No account, no email, no phone number, no password. Open the page, get your answer, close the tab. There is nothing to delete afterwards because there is nothing to delete.
  • Your figures are never written down Income, debts and credit score exist for the length of one calculation. They are not saved, not logged, and not recoverable by anyone, including us.
  • Nobody is watching you use it No analytics, no advertising, no cookie banner, no session tracking, and no third-party code of any kind loading in the background.
  • Your details are never sold or shared Basis makes no money from your data and passes nothing to any lender. No bank on your results has been told you exist, and none of them will contact you.

Built to need almost nothing

There is no field for your name, your date of birth, your address, your account numbers or your social security number, because none of them are needed to answer the question you came with. The only thing that leaves this server is your ZIP code, on its own, so that nearby banks can be found.
