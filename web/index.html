Most loan sites hand you a rate and a lead form. Basis hands you the whole picture, and shows the arithmetic behind every line of it.

Every program you qualify for

FHA, VA, USDA, conventional, jumbo, auto, personal and SBA, each checked against its published federal or GSE standard and ranked by what it costs you a month.

The exact number behind every answer

Not "declined". Instead: you are 60 points short of 620, or $13,799 more down brings your loan-to-value inside the cap. Every check names your value and the limit it met.

Real banks, ranked

The actual FDIC-insured institutions with branches in your ZIP code, ordered by proximity and by how many people complained about them while applying for a loan.

04

What you could borrow

Your true ceiling on income and debts alone, the monthly payment room behind it, and the date the money would realistically land after weekends and bank holidays.
