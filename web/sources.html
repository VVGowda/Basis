Sources.

Basis holds no opinions of its own. Every institution, every threshold and every rate comes from a public authority, read fresh rather than stored and left to rot.

Which banks exist

Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation

The federal register of insured institutions. Who is active, how large they are, and where their branches physically sit. This is why the banks on your results are real ones with real addresses.

What they are like

Consumer Financial Protection Bureau

The federal consumer complaint record. How many people raised a complaint against each bank, whether it happened while applying or while repaying, and whether the bank answered in time.

What money costs

United States Treasury

The government's own reported average interest rate. Consumer lending is priced off this benchmark, so every rate estimate on Basis starts from the real one rather than a guess.

The rules themselves

HUD, VA, USDA, Fannie Mae and the SBA

The published underwriting standards behind FHA, VA, rural, conventional and small business lending. The same documents a loan officer works from, encoded check by check.

Currency

European Central Bank

Official daily reference rates. If you earn in a currency other than dollars, your figures are converted at the published rate before a single ratio is calculated.

Timing

Federal holiday calendar

The days US banks are closed, so a funding estimate counts working days and never lands on a date the money could not actually move.

Read fresh.

Rates move, banks merge, branches close and complaint records grow. A figure that was right last quarter is not good enough.

On every request

Live, not archived

The authorities above are queried when you press the button, not copied into a database months ago and quietly left to drift out of date.

On every result

Told to your face

Your results name which sources answered and whether each was live or briefly cached, so you always know how current the answer in front of you is.

Always

Never invented

Where a figure is an estimate rather than a published fact, Basis says so on the line itself. Nothing is dressed up as more certain than it is.

Why this is the whole point

Most loan sites show you a rate and ask for your phone number. The rate came from somewhere, but you are never told where, and the number goes to whoever pays the most for it. Basis is built the opposite way round: the authority behind every figure is named, and nobody is paying for your details, because your details are never collected.
